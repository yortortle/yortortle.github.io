Chapter 7 Season 1: Pacific Break

FORTNITE BATTLE ROYALE · v39.00 · NOV 29, 2025

The launch of Chapter 7 on a new island, the Golden Coast. The season added a new weapon pool, wingsuit mobility, self-revive and new down-but-not-out options, then ran Winterfest and a South Park collaboration during the season.

What I Shipped

  • Twin Hammer Shotguns: Built the dual-wielded shotguns, with each hand firing independently.
  • Iron Pump Shotgun & Deadeye Assault Rifle: Engineering owner for the Chapter's new core pump shotgun and the scoped Deadeye AR with its unique recoil pattern.
  • Self-Revive Device: Engineering owner for the Self-Revive Device, one of the headline gameplay changes of Chapter 7, which lets downed players get themselves back up.
  • Kenny's Respawn Token (South Park): Built the Mythic Medallion from the South Park collab that brings players back after elimination. Players can't take damage while respawning, their score and quest progress carry through, and it works correctly alongside the Self-Revive Device.
  • Invisibility Goo: Programmed the radioactive goo in Classified Canyon's secret Area 51 base, which cloaks players who step into it.
  • Sneaky Snowman: Made a new version of the Sneaky Snowman for Winterfest.
  • Winterfest Returning Items: Brought back Ice Skates, Sgt. Winter, the Med-Mist Grenade, Chug Splash, Pizza Party and the Shield Keg.

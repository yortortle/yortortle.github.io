Treasure Hunters

FORTNITE MINI SEASON · v43.00 · NOV 1, 2026

A pirate-themed mini season built around the One Piece collaboration, set on the new 80-player Crossbones Isle. Players could take on Devil Fruit powers and turn into Luffy or Ace, collect a new set of Sprites, and face A$AP Rocky as the season's first boss.

What I Shipped

  • Gum-Gum Fruit (Luffy Mythic): Full engineering ownership of the Mythic that turns players into Monkey D. Luffy: the charged Gum Gum Pistol stretch punch and melee combos, the Gum Gum Rocket launch, which finds two anchor points on the ground and slingshots the player across the island, and the Gum Gum Battleaxe mid-air ground slam. I tuned the launch across slopes, backward movement and tight spaces, and fixed IK on the stretched arms so they track their targets.
  • A$AP Rocky's Shock Rocket: Engineered the Exotic rocket launcher that the A$AP Rocky boss wields on Crossbones Isle. It drops to players, and its shock charges can be spent on a jump boost.
  • Sprites Collection: Owned the season's Sprite collection: the Sprite Dex with seasonal numbering, chase and secret Sprites, badges stamped on Victory Royales, and a new pity system that guarantees Sprites from season chests.
  • Transponder Snail Sprite: Implemented the One Piece Den Den Mushi Sprite, which grants extra resources on pickup and shares a bonus with teammates who have it equipped.
